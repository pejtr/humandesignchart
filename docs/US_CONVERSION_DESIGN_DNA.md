# HumanDesignChart.app — US Conversion Design DNA

Status: implementation brief for `feat/us-premium-conversion-home`.

## Positioning
HumanDesignChart.app is a premium self-discovery product for the US market, not a generic mystical landing page. The free chart is the acquisition engine; paid depth and ongoing guidance create LTV.

## Subject / POV
**Your unique design, translated into decisions you can use.**

The product should feel precise, intimate, calm and premium. The bodygraph is the hero product artifact. Avoid unverifiable authority signals and invented reviews.

## Typography
Editorial serif for emotional headlines; restrained grotesk/system sans for product UI, labels, data and forms. Strong contrast between editorial promise and analytical chart data.

## Hero
Headline direction: **Your Unique Design for a More Aligned Life**.
Support copy explains what the chart reveals without promising deterministic outcomes.
Primary CTA: **Get My Free Chart**.
Microcopy: **Free chart · No credit card · Instant results**.

Hero visual is the actual bodygraph/result UI, not decorative stock imagery.

## Conversion architecture
1. Free chart.
2. Instant useful result: Type, Strategy, Authority, Profile.
3. Contextual preview of deeper interpretation.
4. Deep Reading offer.
5. Pro layer for recurring guidance/transits/relationship and career insights.

Pricing hypotheses must remain experiments until validated; do not hard-code fabricated savings, review counts or media logos.

## Motion
One orchestrated moment: bodygraph nodes/centers reveal progressively after the hero enters, followed by the four result chips. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion`. No constant distracting particles on mobile.

## Mobile-first rules
- Primary CTA visible without scrolling on common phone heights.
- Minimum 44px interactive targets.
- Birth form is one clear vertical flow.
- Bodygraph remains legible and never becomes decorative noise.
- No horizontal overflow at 375px.

## Anti-patterns
- Generic purple galaxy / horoscope template.
- Fake press logos, fake testimonials, fake user counts.
- Stock-photo-first hero.
- Multiple competing hero CTAs.
- Spiritual certainty or health/financial claims.
- Hiding the actual chart until after a paywall.
