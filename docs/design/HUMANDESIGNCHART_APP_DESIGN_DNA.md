# HumanDesignChart.app — Design DNA

Status: **canonical**  
Domain: **https://www.humandesignchart.app**  
Primary market: **United States / English-speaking international**  
Purpose: premium self-discovery product optimized for paid acquisition, free-chart activation, Deep Reading conversion, and recurring LTV.

## 1. Brand position

HumanDesignChart.app is **not** presented as a generic spirituality site.

The product should feel like:

- a premium self-discovery product;
- a modern personal insight dashboard;
- editorial luxury rather than “mystic template”;
- credible, calm, precise and emotionally resonant;
- useful before it is decorative.

Reference feel: **Calm × Linear × premium editorial astrology × quantified-self dashboard**.

### Core promise

> Your Unique Design for a More Aligned Life

Supporting copy should focus on:

- understanding personal patterns;
- decision-making reflection;
- relationships;
- practical self-knowledge;
- personalization from birth data.

Avoid deterministic language such as “unlock your destiny”, “guaranteed life purpose”, “predict your future”, and any medical, psychological, financial or diagnostic claims.

Human Design is framed as a **self-reflection / self-discovery framework**, not clinical science or guaranteed prediction.

## 2. Canonical visual world

### Palette

| Token | HEX | Usage |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Midnight Ink | #071426 | hero, footer, primary dark canvas |
| Deep Navy | #081A31 | elevated dark surfaces |
| Observatory Navy | #0D2442 | cards, secondary surfaces |
| Solar Gold | #F4C762 | primary CTA, active accent, icons |
| Deep Gold | #C99534 | secondary gold / hover / details |
| Warm Cream | #F7F3EA | primary light background |
| Paper White | #FBF8F1 | cards / editorial sections |
| Iris Violet | #8B5CF6 | subtle energetic accent only |
| Slate Copy | #66707D | secondary light-mode body copy |

Rules:

- Navy + warm cream carry about 80% of the visual system.
- Gold is the commercial/action accent.
- Violet is a restrained energy accent, never a full-page generic gradient.
- Avoid rainbow chakra palettes except inside the bodygraph artifact itself.

### Typography

Primary editorial headline:
- high-contrast serif;
- large, elegant, compact tracking;
- italic serif only for one emotional phrase such as **Aligned Life**.

Product/UI copy:
- clean neutral grotesk/sans;
- compact labels;
- strong weight contrast;
- no oversized all-caps except small eyebrow labels.

Hierarchy:
- hero H1: 46–76 px responsive;
- section H2: 36–52 px;
- card title: 24–32 px;
- body: 15–18 px;
- eyebrow: 10–12 px with wide tracking.

### Shape language

- main cards: 24–30 px radius;
- CTA: 12–14 px radius;
- subtle 1 px borders;
- deep soft shadows, not glossy neumorphism;
- dark glass panels only over imagery / hero artifact.

## 3. Hero composition — mandatory

Desktop uses an asymmetric two-column composition.

### Left

- eyebrow: SCIENCE · WISDOM · SELF-DISCOVERY;
- headline: Your Unique Design for a More Aligned Life;
- supporting copy;
- three trust points:
  - Personalized to your birth data
  - Instant chart calculation
  - Private — your data stays yours
- primary CTA: Get My Free Chart
- reassurance: Free chart · No credit card · Instant results

### Right

A premium visual artifact combining:

1. human portrait / aspirational lifestyle imagery;
2. visible Human Design bodygraph;
3. an example chart snapshot:
   - Type
   - Strategy
   - Authority
   - Profile
4. a CTA to calculate the visitor's own chart.

The bodygraph is **the hero product**, not a decorative icon.

### Hero imagery

The approved reference direction is:

- cinematic golden-hour portrait;
- coastal / Mediterranean warmth;
- intimate but premium;
- no generic “woman meditating in galaxy” stock art;
- no overproduced fantasy aura effects;
- chart UI layered as real product information.

## 4. Responsive DNA

### Desktop ≥ 1024 px

- max-width about 1440 px;
- two-column hero;
- generous negative space;
- bodygraph + product data visible simultaneously;
- navigation visible;
- primary CTA in nav and hero;
- discovery grid 5 columns.

### Tablet 768–1023 px

- hero remains premium but compresses into a stacked or 55/45 composition;
- headline remains editorial;
- CTA stays above fold;
- bodygraph artifact remains visible, never reduced to a tiny icon;
- discovery grid can wrap.

### Mobile < 768 px

Order is sacred:

1. brand header;
2. headline;
3. short promise;
4. primary CTA;
5. reassurance;
6. trust points;
7. chart/product visual;
8. discovery cards;
9. how it works;
10. premium offer.

On mobile:
- no side-by-side micro-columns that create unreadable cards;
- sticky conversion CTA may be added later if measured to help;
- primary CTA minimum height 52–56 px;
- avoid popups before user has seen the free-chart value proposition.

## 5. Conversion architecture

Primary acquisition funnel:

    ChatGPT Ads / Search / Organic
            ↓
    Free Human Design Chart
            ↓
    Instant personalized result
            ↓
    Deep Reading
            ↓
    Annual / recurring premium
            ↓
    Relationship / career / transit / additional readings

The home page sells **the free chart**, not the subscription.

The result experience sells **the deeper interpretation**.

The paid report / customer portal sells **ongoing value**.

### Primary CTA

Canonical:
- Get My Free Chart

Secondary:
- See what your chart says
- Explore the Deep Reading
- See premium options

Do not split attention with equal-weight CTAs in the hero.

## 6. Paid-acquisition economics encoded in the design

The design must support:

- low-friction free activation;
- measurable free-chart completion;
- immediate personalized value;
- clear Deep Reading upsell;
- recurring-LTV upsell after value is demonstrated.

Primary conversion events to instrument:

1. landing_view
2. free_chart_started
3. free_chart_completed
4. deep_reading_viewed
5. checkout_started
6. purchase_confirmed
7. annual_upgrade

Do not optimize paid campaigns on a superficial page view when deeper events are available.

## 7. Proof and trust rules

Do not invent:

- customer counts;
- review counts;
- press mentions;
- star ratings;
- scientific validation;
- clinical outcomes.

A visual reference may show generic “as seen in” or review modules, but production UI must only display them when backed by real evidence.

Preferred trust mechanisms:

- transparent product screenshots;
- exact explanation of what the chart contains;
- privacy language;
- real verified testimonials;
- refund / fulfillment clarity;
- clear payment provider;
- visible free-chart experience.

## 8. Content density

The approved design intentionally has **high information density with strong hierarchy**.

Every major section should answer one of these questions:

- What is this?
- What do I get?
- What makes my result personal?
- How fast can I try it?
- What will the paid report add?
- What happens after purchase?

Avoid decorative sections that do not move comprehension or conversion.

## 9. Motion

One orchestration moment per viewport.

Preferred motion:

- subtle bodygraph glow / line pulse;
- tiny gold highlight movement;
- restrained image parallax;
- card lift 2–4 px;
- fade/slide-in when sections enter viewport.

Avoid:

- constant particle storms;
- looping cosmic backgrounds;
- excessive scroll-jacking;
- multiple competing animation systems.

Motion must never reduce Core Web Vitals or readability.

## 10. UX / accessibility

Required:

- AA contrast for text;
- visible keyboard focus;
- 44 px minimum touch target;
- descriptive alt text where imagery has meaning;
- reduced-motion support;
- no essential information encoded by color alone;
- form errors inline and explicit;
- date/time/place input optimized for mobile.

## 11. Anti-patterns — banned for HumanDesignChart.app

Do not revert to:

- generic purple spiritual gradients;
- floating sacred-geometry decoration as the main hero;
- “unlock your destiny” copy;
- generic chakra illustrations as product proof;
- fake reviews or fake media logos;
- one-size-fits-all pricing cards above the free-chart CTA;
- generic stock photos unrelated to the actual product;
- low-density template SaaS sections;
- hero without a visible bodygraph / product artifact.
